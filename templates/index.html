🔬 How BB84 Detects Eve

1
Alice creates bits
Alice randomly generates 0 and 1 values and randomly selects quantum bases.
2
Bob measures
Bob independently chooses random bases to measure the incoming quantum states.
3
Calculate QBER
Alice and Bob compare bases and estimate the error rate from selected matching positions.
4
Detect disturbance
If the estimated QBER is too high, the key is rejected and secure communication stops.
